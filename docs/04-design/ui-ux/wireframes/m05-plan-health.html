ENGLISH (en-US)

9:41▮▮▮ ◔ ▭

Plan Health

✓ Good — ready to save
Every stop is open at its planned time, every leg has a route, and each day ends before 18:00.

Health is recalculated after every change. Real opening hours can change; check the place card before you go.

Specification notes

Purpose and persona goal

Tom understands exactly why the day does not fit and fixes it before going (US-015).

Components

HealthSummary (good, warning, blocked), IssueCard, Badge, Action (teal small, secondary small).

Interaction

Reached from the health badge on SCR-024/025 or from a Save refusal (SCR-029). Go to stop → SCR-024 with the stop selected; fix shortcuts open SCR-027 prefilled.

States

StateTreatment
LoadingRecalculating banner; previous result greyed with “Đang cập nhật / Updating”.
EmptyGood state is the empty state.
UnknownListed as warning, not hidden.
StaleListed as warning with age in days.
BlockedAs shown.
ErrorIf validation cannot run: “Không kiểm tra được kế hoạch / Could not check the plan”, Save disabled.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Severity conveyed by icon, word and position, not colour alone (1.4.1); headings for “Must fix” and “Worth checking”; each issue is a group with its own actions.
