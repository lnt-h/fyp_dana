Specification notes

Purpose and persona goal

Tom reviews a proposed day as an ordered timeline with real legs, sees what blocks it and decides what to change before saving.

Components

New: StopCard (default, selected, warning, blocked, locked), LegRow (walk, drive, unavailable), HealthBadge, UnsavedBadge, DayTabs, SegmentedControl, Sheet. Built: Action.

Interaction

Tap Plan Health badge → SCR-026. Tap ⋯ on a stop → SCR-027. Timeline/Map toggle keeps the selected stop (US-014 AC2). Save → SCR-029. ⋯ in app bar → Regenerate day (SCR-030), Delete draft.

States

StateTreatment
LoadingSkeleton stop cards on first open; “Recalculating…” badge after edits.
EmptyEmpty day card as shown.
UnknownBadge “Giờ mở cửa: chưa rõ / Opening hours: unknown” (warning), never blank.
StaleBadge “có thể đã cũ / may be out of date” when verified > 180 days ago.
BlockedRed left edge + reason at stop; health badge lists count.
ErrorBanner, edits kept (US-019 AC2).
OfflineRead-only; edits and Save disabled with reason.
Superseded/expiredBanner: “Bản nháp đã hết hạn / This draft expired” with Create again.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Status never by colour alone: icon + text in every badge; stop cards are list items with name, time and status in one accessible label; reorder also possible via Edit stop (move up/down) — no drag-only interaction (WCAG 2.5.1 alternative); focus order: header, health, view toggle, day tabs, stops, Save.
